# Glossary

Definitions of various Words and terms.

[P](#p)  [R](#r)

---

## P

### Path

An ordered list of two or more [points](Glossary#point) specifying a path through space.  Usually points are 2D.

### Point

A list of numbers, also called a vector.  Usually has length 2 or 3 to represent points in the place on points in space.

### Pointlist

An unordered list of [points](Glossary#point).

### Polygon

A [path](Glossary#path), usually 2D, that describes a polygon by asuming that the first and last point are connected.

## R

### Region

A list of one or more non-intersecting [polygons](Glossary#polygon) representing a union of one or more disconnected polygons that may have internal holes.
